# Safety-critical facts require human verification

Exact specifications and compatibility rules are checked against their original manufacturer documents before they can support an answer, even when an LLM proposes their extraction. Answers also fail closed when a claim lacks a verified source, a procedure cannot be established as complete, or a number cannot be matched to evidence. This reduces corpus coverage, but it protects the credibility of the project's accuracy claims and prevents plausible extraction errors from becoming answers.
